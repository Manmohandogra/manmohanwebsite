About

A Voice of
Banaras

Rooted in the revered Banaras Gharana and shaped by two decades of dedicated practice, Manmohan Dogra is one of the most compelling voices in Hindustani classical percussion today — every dha, tun and na ringing with crystalline precision.

A disciple of SNA Awardee Pt. Vijay Shankar Mishra, he has broadened his rhythmic palette through the Delhi and Ajrada traditions — one of the few artists who fluently speaks the language of multiple tabla gharanas, from Edinburgh Festival Fringe to the Nehru Centre in London.
