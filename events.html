From the Archive · Projects

Talayan — A Rhythm Project

Not a single concert but an ongoing project — the eye of rhythm, exploring new collaborations beyond the concert stage.

The first chapter, Talayan: Eye of Rhythm, brought three traditions — tabla, sitar and Jazz drums — onto a single stage at OMNOM Islington. That evening now lives on as a record of the night, and the project moves forward as the Talayan Festival, with 2.0 coming soon.
